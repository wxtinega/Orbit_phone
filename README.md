# T.I Dial

A full phone app for Android with a clean glass design, your real contacts and call history, and a few things other dialers don't do.

## Highlights
- **Your phone app:** set T.I Dial as the default phone app and it shows incoming calls the moment they ring, even on the lock screen, with the caller's photo.
- **Two dial pads:** Orbit (keys around a ring) or Classic (the 3 x 4 grid). Switch any time in Settings.
- **Gesture Speed Dial:** drag across three or more keys to call someone instantly.
- **In-call controls:** mute, keypad, speaker, Bluetooth and headset picker, hold, add call, merge, swap, conference management.
- **Notes after calls,** and optional call recording (Settings > Call recording).
- **Contacts:** photos you can set from your gallery, per-contact ringtones, favourites, nickname and keypad search, WhatsApp and Messages buttons, blocking.
- **Recents:** missed, incoming and outgoing filters, call insights, notes shown beside the call.
- **Looks:** orange accent (or five others), light, dark or automatic, glass that lets your wallpaper show through.

Everything stays on the phone.

## Install T.I Dial
1. On your Android phone (Android 8 or newer), open this link: **https://github.com/wxtinega/Orbit_phone/releases/download/app/TI-Dial.apk**
2. Open the downloaded `TI-Dial.apk`. If Android asks, allow installing apps from your browser or Files app.
3. Tap **Install**, then open **T.I Dial**.
4. Tap **Get started**, allow the permissions, and set T.I Dial as your **default phone app** when asked. Without that, it can't show incoming calls or caller photos.

**Updating:** open the same link again and tap **Update**. Your settings, notes and recordings stay.

You can also scan this link as a QR code or share it in a message. No account is needed.

## Who can change it
Anyone can download and use T.I Dial, and read this code. Only the owner can change this repository or publish updates: builds are signed with a key kept in the owner's private GitHub secrets, so a modified copy built by someone else can't install over yours.
